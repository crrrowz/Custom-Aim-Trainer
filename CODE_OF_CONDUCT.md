# Code of Conduct

AimForge is built by and for players with very different skill levels, languages and backgrounds.
Everyone taking part is expected to make that a good experience.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers; everyone's aim and code started somewhere.
- Accept that decisions about measurement are made on evidence, and bring evidence when you disagree.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sexual content or unwelcome attention.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may
block people who repeatedly do so. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through
GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
