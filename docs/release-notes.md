First public alpha of AimForge, a VALORANT aim trainer that finds what is wrong with your aim and
checks whether practice really fixed it.

## Download

- **`AimForge-*-windows-x64-setup.exe`**: installer for your user, no admin rights needed.
- **`AimForge-*-windows-x64-portable.zip`**: unzip and run `AimForge.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11 only. The builds are not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn: choose
**More info → Run anyway**. Check files against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## What is in it

- An assessment of eleven aim skills, with concrete findings such as systematic overshoot or clicking
  before arrival.
- A six-day plan built around your top two problems, with drills that adapt to your results, then a
  retest that separates real improvement from getting used to the test.
- Per-session analysis: aim replay of every target, shot map, accuracy and speed by direction.
- Progress charts, free practice, and coach reports written offline or by an AI provider you choose.
- Arabic and English.

## Known limitations

- Skill norms are provisional; read scores as change over time, not as a rank.
- Movement values for counter-strafe drills are provisional.
- API keys for AI providers are stored unencrypted on your computer.
